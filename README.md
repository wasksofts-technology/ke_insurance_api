# ke_insurance_api
Insurance APIs in Kenya enable digital integration  to InsurTech &amp; Commercial APIs for intermediaries. Insurance API integration in Kenya is primarily built around embedded insurance and mobile-money distribution, with the market shifting toward "invisible" coverage embedded into everyday transactions.

## Digital Motor Insurance for all insurance company in kenya

The most mature and standardized API integration is the Digital Motor Vehicle Insurance Certificate (DMVIC) system, which is mandatory for motor insurance. What it does: Issues, validates, cancels, and manages motor insurance certificates electronically for  issuance types A, B, C, and D 

## Key compliance considerations
Data Protection: Health information is sensitive; controller/processor registration applies under the 2019 legal framework

Payment Regulation: FinTechs in payments must be authorized as Payment Service Providers (PSP) by the Central Bank of Kenya

Security: API integrations handling identity, payment, and claims data face rising cyber threats (detected threats increased 80.8% QoQ in late 2024)
